<div lang="zh">

人类天生有逐利心理，会自然地去寻找收益，种田，进厂打螺丝，去大厂上班或者去开公司，只是在不同逐利预期下，根据手头的先天、后天的资源，去选择的不同投入产出项目。公司会因为员工离去而消失，但先有成熟的商业模式，后才有聚集起来的员工。矿工也是如此，因协议的可行性聚集，通过投入资源，获得收益。

</div>

<div lang="en">

Humans are born profit-seeking. They look for returns as a matter of course. Farming the land, turning screws in a factory, taking a job at a big tech firm, starting a company of your own — these are only different input-and-output propositions, chosen under different expectations of return, according to the innate and acquired resources at hand. A company disappears when its employees leave, but the mature business model comes first, and only then do the employees gather around it. Miners are the same. They gather because the protocol is viable. They put in resources, and they take out returns.

</div>
