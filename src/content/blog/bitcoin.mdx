## Miners and the Bitcoin network

Symbiotic: miners joining is what makes the network's decentralization real,
and miners leaving puts security at risk.

Causal: the decentralized protocol design is the cause, and has value in
itself; the market's pricing of the coin is the effect, creating a profit
opportunity that draws miners in.

I lean causal.

Humans are born profit-seeking. They look for returns as a matter of course. Deliver doordash orders, taking a big tech job, launching an SF AI startup — these are only different input-and-output propositions, chosen under different expectations of return, according to the innate and acquired resources at hand. A company disappears when its employees leave, but the mature business model comes first, and only then do the employees gather around it. Miners are the same. They gather because the protocol is viable. They put in resources, and they take out returns.
Like many industry disappeared historically, yes the people coexist with the industry, but it's the time and value that industry provides that gathers people. As long as bitcoin's value proposition is valid. It will have it.
